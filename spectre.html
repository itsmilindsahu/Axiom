Spectre reads a spectrum backwards.

Spectral databases tell you which spectrum matches a molecule. Spectre goes the other way: from a raw, unlabelled infrared spectrum to the functional groups that produced it, with every prediction pointing to specific peaks.

Illustrative infrared spectrum. Move over a peak to see its group.

How it works

A rule engine built on infrared correlation tables works alongside a 1D convolutional network trained on synthetic data. The pipeline handles ingestion, baseline correction, smoothing and peak-shape features, and includes a digitizer that reads spectra from PNG or JPG plots.

Status

The rule-based classifier and the 1D network are built. An NMR module is planned next, with multi-modal structure elucidation as the longer goal.

Open problems

Labelled NMR data, validation against real lab spectra, and more functional-group classes. Chemists and spectroscopists are especially welcome. Get in touch.
