Theme: Constrained Stochastic Modelling Preprint

Volatility surfaces that cannot be arbitraged.

Standard methods fit an implied volatility surface first and then smooth or project it to remove arbitrage. We put the no-arbitrage constraints into the covariance structure of a Gaussian process, so every interpolated surface already respects them.

Arbitrage-free Gaussian process interpolation for implied volatility surfaces. Milind Sahu, Darun, Latheesh Roy. August 2026.

Theme: Inverse Problems & Spectroscopy Open Source · MIT In-Browser Demo

Spectre reads a spectrum backwards.

Spectral databases tell you which spectrum matches a molecule. Spectre goes the other way: from a raw infrared spectrum to the functional groups that produced it. A rule engine built on correlation tables works alongside a 1D convolutional network, and every prediction points back to specific peaks.

Runs fully client-side with WebAssembly/JS. No backend dependency. NMR extension currently in research.

Theme: Signal Disaggregation & Energy Systems Competition Laurels

Measuring energy and waste.

Our NILM system infers appliance-level energy use from one meter. TRACEWASTE treats waste as a chain-of-custody proof, while EcoFusion senses appliance energy optically.
