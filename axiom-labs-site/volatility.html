Volatility surfaces that cannot be arbitraged.

Standard methods fit an implied volatility surface first, then smooth or project it to remove arbitrage. We put the no-arbitrage constraints into the covariance of a Gaussian process, so every interpolated surface already respects them.

Illustration: a Gaussian process surface. Move over it.

Authors
Milind Sahu, Darun, Latheesh Roy
Status
Preprint, August 2026
Paper
10.13140/RG.2.2.33170.62400

Why it matters

Post-hoc smoothing can distort prices that traders rely on. Building the constraints in means no projection step, and the model still reports its own uncertainty.

Open problems

Shape constraints such as monotonicity and convexity in the covariance of other surfaces. Optimal transport kernels for regression on distributions. If either interests you, get in touch.
