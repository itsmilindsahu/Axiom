Measuring energy and waste.

Three systems that infer what happened from limited signals, and a preprint arguing that energy disaggregation should be public infrastructure.

Illustration: one meter reading is the sum of every appliance.

NILM

Appliance-level energy use inferred from a single meter. We treat it as supervised time-series classification, with random forests for anomaly detection and an LSTM for multi-step load prediction. The pipeline runs from ESP32 hardware through a FastAPI backend to a web dashboard.

1st Prize, NIT Meghalaya Hackathon. 3rd Prize, Inter-IISER Entrepreneurial Summit (IIES) 2026.

Code: BHARAT-NILM  ·  dashboard

TRACEWASTE

Waste accountability as a chain-of-custody proof. QR-linked batch records, geo-tagged verification events and timestamped logs make each handover checkable. Deployed as an offline-capable web app.

2nd Prize, Eco Champion Hackathon, IIT Hyderabad

Live demo and code

EcoFusion

Optical sensing of appliance energy use, with cost forecasting and anomaly detection. Built end to end by one person.

Rank 5, Hack Beyond Limits 2025, among 1,000+ participants
